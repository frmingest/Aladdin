# 3D S26 PR 3: the farmhouse and the fish farm (2026-11-03)

**Status: written and tested (tsc, eslint, vitest). The picture below was rendered in headless Chrome with software graphics from a throwaway page, not from `/game3d`; the real land was NOT opened in a browser, draw calls were not measured, not merged, not deployed.** Plan: [S26 sprint plan](game3d-s26-dressings-sprint-plan-2026-11-03.md), "PR 3". Builds on S26 PR 1 (#281).

## What you will see

Walk into a tower whose holding is **food** (the sector table's farm theme) and the middle of its land is a **farmhouse in a fenced yard** with grain fields, instead of S25's plain tower. The gate stone, lanterns, records house and price post are unchanged.

**How it wears the wall tier** (from the one ladder, wall tier alone):

| Rung | Farm |
|---|---|
| Sound | upright house, whole fence, all four fields in grain, a full hedge at the front |
| Solid | the same with a small plain patch and a lower hedge |
| Worn | a slight lean, two fence segments missing, one field bare, one prop |
| Patched | a clear lean, four fence gaps, two bare fields, three props, loose boards, a fallen log |
| Failing | strong lean, a crack across the front, seven fence gaps, all four fields bare, broken props, a heap of fallen lumber |
| Ghost / Neutral | drawn by the slot, as before (pale block in mist / plain block) |

The stream round the yard is the shared band from the moat tier (S26 PR 1). Nothing about the sector, weight, gain or verdict changes how worn the farm is.

**The fish farm.** If the holding's sector or name says fish, salmon, seafood, trout or aquaculture (`isFishFarm` in `farmDressing.ts`), the two back fields become three round open tanks with four silver-blue fish each. The numbers are fixed (3 tanks, 12 fish): they never grow or shrink with any value. Fish are silver-blue (`#a9bfd2`, `#8fa9c2`), never red, orange or green; a test checks that.

Picture: [game3d-s26-farm-2026-11-03.png](game3d-s26-farm-2026-11-03.png): a ghost far left, then Sound, Worn, Failing, then a fish farm at Solid on the right, each with its stream band. Software render, flat lighting. It shows the rungs reading clearly; the bare fields look reddish-brown (the pack's dirt colour, which passes the pack colour audit) and the hedge is green (leaf green stays green by decision).

**Visual verb + scene:** *visit the holding's workplace*, standing in a farm yard.

## Truth rules kept

- Condition from the wall tier only (`farmLook(condition)` takes the condition alone), theme from the sector, stream from the moat.
- No pile or count grows with a value: tanks and fish are constants; fence, field and lumber counts come from the rung only.
- Condition is lean, gaps and rubble, never colour. No red or green is introduced by code.
- Still: nothing here moves, so reduced motion changes nothing.

## Code

- `game3d/farmDressing.ts` (pure: `farmLook`, `isFishFarm`, layout constants) and `farmDressing.test.ts`.
- `game3d/FarmDressing.tsx` (pieces from `holding-farm.glb`, Builder `house`, hedges from `town-nature.glb`; fish tanks code-built; one `StaticBatch`; plain block as fallback if a model fails to load).
- `landCentre.ts`: `DRESSING_BUILT.farm = true`, and the slot carries `fish`. `LandCentre.tsx` draws the farm for the farm theme. `landCentre.test.ts` now checks "no dressing" only for themes not built.
