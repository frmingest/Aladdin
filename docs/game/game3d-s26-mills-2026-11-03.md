# 3D S26 PR 2: the windmill and the watermill (2026-11-03)

**Status: written and tested (tsc, lint, 1553 frontend tests, build). The picture below was rendered in headless Chrome with software graphics from a throwaway page, not from the game. The land itself was NOT opened in a browser; draw calls and fps not measured; not merged, not deployed.** Plan: [S26 sprint plan](game3d-s26-dressings-sprint-plan-2026-11-03.md), "PR 2". Builds on S26 PR 1 (#281).

## What you will see

Walk into a tower door whose holding has the **windmill** or **watermill** theme (energy and utilities on the flat Map; in the demo set only Exxon Mobil) and the land's centre is a mill instead of S25's tower. The land is laid out as before (gate stone, lanterns, records house, price post); the stream band round the centre is the moat tier, drawn by the slot.

- **Condition (wall tier only).** Sound: upright, stone footing, a tidy half ring of low fence. Worn: slats missing, a lean, a prop. Failing: strong lean, a crack across the front, broken props, loose boards, fallen stones, most of the fence gone. Not surveyed is the pale ghost and no walls is the neutral block: both are drawn once by the slot, so neither mill draws them.
- **Windmill** stands still (decision G1). **Watermill** keeps its real water tile as scenery; it never carries the moat fact.
- **Scenery** (sacks, a barrel, a crate, a wheelbarrow or a bucket) is identical on every rung and never grows with any value.

Picture: [game3d-s26-mills-2026-11-03.png](game3d-s26-mills-2026-11-03.png): windmill at Sound, Worn and Failing, then the watermill at Sound and Failing, then the ghost (left to right, each with a different stream). Software render, flat test lights; the real light and sky are untested.

**Visual verb + scene:** *visit the holding's workplace*: standing in a mill yard.

## Truth rules kept

- The plan (`millPlan(theme, condition)`) takes the ladder's condition and nothing else. Theme only picks which mill; sector, weight, size, margin of safety and verdict change nothing.
- Unknown stays fog (ghost); no red or green (pieces are the packed KayKit files, already colour-audited; code-built footing, patches and crack use stone, timber and ink).
- Condition is shape, lean and rubble, never colour. Nothing moves, so reduced motion changes nothing.

## Code

- `game3d/millDressing.ts` (pure plan + tests in `millDressing.test.ts`), `MillDressing.tsx` (draws it from `holding-windmill.glb` / `holding-watermill.glb`).
- `LandCentre.tsx` gets a `DRESSINGS` table, one line per theme; `DRESSING_BUILT.windmill/watermill` flipped in `landCentre.ts`; `landCentre.test.ts` no longer says "no dressing yet".
- **Gotcha for the next dressings:** in the packed files the scene root is named like the main building (`windmill`) and the loader renames the real node to `windmill_1`, so `getObjectByName("windmill")` returns the whole file. `MillDressing` looks among the file's top-level children (name, then name + `_1`).
