## Answers to the open questions on #380

- **North-wall dials:** the Weather Tower no longer has clock dials; the issue #402 plates replaced them, and they show a value and a chart. Nothing to judge as "dials".
- **Desktop width:** seen at 1573 px (courtyard, Weather Tower, Siege Field, Town Hall, holding land) and, on 2026-11-09, at a phone-sized pane (712 px).
- **fps:** every place read 146 to 165 fps. 165 is probably the screen's refresh cap, so the real headroom is unknown. This is one desktop GPU; phone frame rate is still not measured.
- **"Town at night":** there is no night mode in the 3D code. The sky only changes with the weather look (calm, gathering, besieged, and a pale mist when the siege level is not surveyed, `weatherPlan.ts`), which follows the siege level. A "Town at night" look cannot be tested as written; it needs a decision (drop it, or test the Town under the besieged sky).

## What is still true but not seen

Records Hall, Royal Mint and Market Quarter; the other three Town Hall boards up close (expected to wrap like the first one); the Library shelf tags up close; reduced motion; sound; any phone frame rate.

## Findings (each is an issue)

1. **#439** Town Hall board tags wrap into a one-word-wide column (the old F3, left over from T4).
2. **#440** Weather Tower floor banners clip: `fitText` in `drawBanner` allows one line and sizes down to 58 px (read from the code); the clipping is what was seen, and the two being too wide at that size is the likely cause, not measured.
3. **#441** `mergeGeometries` in `staticBatch.ts` fails (cause not confirmed; the console message points at a mismatched `uv` type). The browser console showed four pairs of three.js errors per load (an observation; the repo code catches the failure silently at `staticBatch.ts:146-149` and leaves those meshes unjoined). Nothing looks wrong, and frame rate is fine on this machine.
