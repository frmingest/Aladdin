# S21: one map of places (2026-11-02)

**Status: written, not looked at in a browser, not merged.** Branch `feature/game3d-s21-areas`. Nothing should look or feel different; this is groundwork for the fade (S22) and the holding lands (S25).

## What it is for

Before this, "where am I" was four separate switches (in a tower, in the Town, in the Town Hall, in the Hall of Records), copied into the scene, the page, the E key and the prompts. Adding the holding lands, the Siege Field and the Market Quarter on top of that would have been fragile. Now there is one list of places and one value that says which one you are in.

## What changed

- **`game3d/areas.ts`**: the list of places (courtyard, Town, Town Hall, Hall of Records, a tower's library). Each says where you arrive, where its doors are, how it is lit and where "leave" goes. It points at the spawn, door and exit numbers that already existed, so no number moved. `holding`, `siege` and `market` are reserved names, not places yet. `returnSpot` gives the spot you step out onto.
- **`game3d/interiorBox.ts`**: the hall box the library and the Town Hall both declared is now one shared definition.
- **`game3d/areaMachine.ts`**: a small reducer (`enter`, `leave`, `ready`, `fadeDone`). The page holds it with one `useReducer`. `ready` and `fadeDone` do nothing yet; S22 gives them work.
- **`game3d/near.ts`**: `pickNear` (the one "arrive near, leave far" rule) and `standingAt` (the one answer to "what would E do here"). Both the E key and the on-screen prompt read `standingAt`, so a prompt can no longer promise something E does not do.
- **`Scene.tsx`**: takes one `area` instead of `inTown`, `inHall`, `inRecords`; one `NearWatch` replaces the twelve courtyard watchers; one teleport effect replaces four; the indoor or outdoor light comes from the list.
- **`Game3DPage.tsx`**: the four flags are gone (`inTown` and friends are now read from the area), the E handler switches on `standingAt`, and the twenty hand-gated prompt blocks became one.

## Small differences you might notice

When two spots overlap in the courtyard, the prompt now always shows the one E will use (before, a few prompt rules disagreed with E). The order E uses is unchanged: person, tower door, desk, drill post, signpost, stand, stall, gate, vault, night watch, ravens, Realm post. A tower's door is still ranked from its wall, so a fat tower is no easier to enter.
