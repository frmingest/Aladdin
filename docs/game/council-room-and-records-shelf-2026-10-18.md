# Council room pictures and bigger Records books (2026-10-18)

**Visual verb + scene:** sit (the Oracle and the Partner at the round table) / shelve (bigger tomes on the Records shelf).

## Council room
- Two wide Stitch pictures replace the small `council-quiet` room: `council-room-quiet` (empty table, nothing on the agenda) and `council-room-busy` (table laid with scrolls, books and candles, items on the agenda). Both 1200x670 WebP, about 55 KB each; masters live in the gitignored `art-masters/Design_master/council-room-2026-10-18`.
- The Oracle and the Partner are painted into the room. Each is a transparent hotspot button over the figure with a gold "Ask the Oracle / Ask the Partner" tag. Pressing one scrolls to "Call someone to the floor"; if a tower is already picked that advisor answers at once, otherwise the line says they are ready and to pick a tower first (no answer is made up).
- With items on the agenda the occupied seats are a row of buttons under the picture and still jump to the agenda item. Art off or picture missing: the old chamber is drawn.
- The hotspots cover the figures only; they carry no fact, count or score.

## Records shelf
- The book on the shelf is as wide as its brass nameplate (both 128 px). The book picture is cropped to the cover so no empty margin makes it look smaller; the nameplate has less padding and smaller type (name up to 3 lines). The open book on the desk is unchanged.
