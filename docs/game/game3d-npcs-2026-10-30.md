# 3D courtyard, week 3: the Oracle and the Partner, and the first quest (2026-10-30)

Status: **written and tested, not merged, not deployed, not yet seen in a browser.** Branch
`feature/game3d-npcs`, off `main` after #199 (week 2) and #200 (look-up fix) were merged.
Frontend only: no backend, migration or prompt change.

## What it does
- Two people stand in the courtyard: the Oracle at the keep door (east side) and the Partner just
  inside the gate (west of the road in). Same two advisors as the flat Fortress. Stand-in figures
  from plain shapes, solid (you walk round them), name floating above.
- Walk within about 3.6 units and a label says "press E". E opens a dialogue panel (and frees the
  mouse so the buttons can be clicked); E or Close ends it; walking away (5.2 units) ends it too.
- The one quest, **Unwritten Thesis**: every holding has a written reason in the decision journal.
  It is **derived, never stored**: `unwrittenThesis()` (`quest.ts`) reads `GET /journal` and counts
  holdings with a non-blank thesis. No save file, no XP, no reward, nothing to drift from the truth.
  All holdings count, not only those on pads.
- **Unknown stays unknown:** while the journal loads the quest says so; if it cannot be read the
  quest and both people say "I cannot read your journal", never "done" and never a guessed count.
  The towers still stand if only the journal fails. Sample (demo) journal is labelled.
- Quest log (left panel) lists the quest, "N of M written", the missing names, and a link to the
  journal. Dialogue is hand-written (`dialogueV1.ts`, chosen by fixed rules from the quest state, no
  model, no randomness): names come from the data, a weight is only quoted when known, no buy/sell
  wording, no cheering. A change to shown wording is a new `dialogueV2.ts`.
