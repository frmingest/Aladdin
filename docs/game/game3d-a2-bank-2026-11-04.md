## What you will see

A holding whose theme is `bank` (financials) now stands as its own building, on the meadow and in its land: a gold chest in the middle, a large, a medium and a small coin stack, a small gold stack and a silver stack, and a writing desk (two short columns under a plank) with an open journal, a pencil, a key and a three-wick candle, all inside a ring of six barred walls (windowed and plain alternate) with the usual stream band outside. The walls wear the wall ladder exactly as the fountain's columns and the bullion barriers do: upright and footed on Sound, leaning and gapped on Worn, props and boards on Patched, fallen pieces and a crack on Failing. Unsurveyed shows the pale ghost. "Not applicable" draws the vault undamaged with no footing, so it never reads as Sound.

## Decision to check (Claude's call)

The bank draws its neutral, like the fountain and the bullion vault. Banks and insurers often have no usable balance-sheet wall tier, so otherwise their usual state would hide behind the plain block. Say if you would rather the neutral stay a block.

## The treasure never moves

Fixed lists, pinned by a test. No count or size of coin, gold or paper depends on the wall, the weight, the size class, a gain, a margin of safety or a verdict. The journal is a prop, not data.
