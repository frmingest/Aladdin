# D2 Camera (2026-10-14)

Status: written on `feature/d2-camera`, not merged, not deployed. From the design-team review ([game-mode-team-review](game-mode-team-review-2026-10-09.md)) and the "Not built" list of [D1](plain-lens-d1-2026-10-13.md). Visual verb + scene: **lean in over the parapet to read a banner.**

## What changed

- **A camera bar under the resource bar** of the painted Fortress: **−**, **+**, **Fit**, **Read labels**, and a line in words ("150% · smallest label 12 px"). Zoom runs from fit (100%) to 400%.
- **Fit** is the old picture: the scene as wide as its frame, never narrower than 720 px, so the phone Fortress **keeps its sideways scroll** (decided 2026-10-13).
- **Read labels** zooms until the smallest label the scene draws (8.5 scene units, the land sign) is **11 px** on screen. When the picture is under 11 px the bar says so ("under 11: press Read labels"). Fit is not forced to 11 px; the floor is one press away, and the number is always stated.
- **Panning** is the frame's own scroll bars. Extra: **mouse drag** pans a zoomed picture (a drag never selects a tower), **Ctrl/Cmd + wheel** zooms at the pointer (a plain wheel still scrolls the page), **+ / − / 0** zoom and fit from the keyboard. Touch keeps native scrolling.
- **Selecting a tower while zoomed** scrolls it into view if it is not fully visible.
- **Survey docked beside the scene on wide screens (>= 1280 px):** the Tower survey, or the verdict on the whole fortress, sits in a 23 rem column to the right of the scene, sticky, so the picture stays in view while you read. Below that width it stays under the scene as before. The Plain view keeps the survey below.

Not changed: the scene's drawing and geometry, the Plain and Ledger views, any stored value, any backend code. No new migration. The camera is per page visit; the zoom is not remembered.

## Files

`frontend/src/lib/sceneCamera.ts` (+ test), `frontend/src/components/fortress/SceneCamera.tsx` (+ test), `frontend/src/pages/FortressPage.tsx` (camera wrapper, docked layout).

## How to check it (Faiz, after deploy)

1. Fortress, Scene. The bar reads 100% and names the smallest label size. Press **Read labels**: the bar reads 11 px and the labels are legible.
2. Press **+** a few times, drag the picture, press **Fit**. Ctrl + wheel over a tower zooms around it.
3. Zoom in, select a tower far from the centre: the picture scrolls to it and the survey opens.
4. On a screen at least 1280 px wide the survey is beside the scene; narrow the window and it drops below.
5. On a phone: the picture scrolls sideways at fit; **Read labels** makes the text readable.
