# S22 PR 1: doors that feel like doors, the fade and the name plate (2026-11-03)

**Status: written, not looked at in a browser, not merged, not deployed.** Branch `feature/game3d-s22-doors`, on `main` `289978d`. Plan: [S22 and S23 sprint plan](game3d-s22-s23-sprint-plan-2026-11-02.md).

## What you will see (plain words)

Press E at a door and the screen goes dark with a thin gold edge, you arrive in the new place, the screen clears, and a name plate says where you are for about two seconds ("Into the Town Hall", "Into Vår Energi's library", "Back in the courtyard"). The whole step is about one second. The browser tab now carries the place's name. With "reduce motion" on, it is a cut: no fade, no slide, the plate still shows.

## What changed

- **`areaMachine.ts`**: a move now walks `fadingOut` (0.35 s, you are still where you were) → `loading` (you are in the new place, screen dark) → `fadingIn` (0.45 s) → `idle`. A second door press during a move is ignored, so presses never stack. If the new place never appears within 3 seconds you are sent back and the plate says "The door is stuck. Try again?"
- **Hold still**: no walking and no E while a move is under way (`hold` into `Player`; the E handler checks `isMoving`).
- **`areaWordsV1.ts`**: the plate lines and the place names (versioned words file, short game voice; a tower names its own holding).
- **`AreaFade.tsx` and `index.css`**: the veil and the plate, in the same gold and parchment as the toolbar.
- **Reduced motion**: `prefersReducedMotion()` makes every move an instant cut.

## Tests (16 new or changed)

Machine: phase order, stays in the old place until dark, no stacking, ready and fadeDone ignored when idle, instant cut, failed load goes back, hold-still per phase. Words: every line six words or fewer, tower named after its holding, plain name for every place.

Checks run in `frontend/`: `tsc --noEmit`, `npm run lint` (2 old warnings), `npm test` (167 files, 1445 tests), `npm run build`.

## Small things to know

- "Loading" is two drawn frames after the move, not a real check that the floor exists: the scene already builds each place's floor the moment the place changes. The 3-second give-up is the safety net.
- Not done in this PR (planned as S22 PR 2): Esc and B, the "← Back" button, `?at=<place>` links with reload, the browser test, and quiet preloading of the next place's models.
