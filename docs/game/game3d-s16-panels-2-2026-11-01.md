## What a sprint is for (plain words)

The flat Fortress has pages with things you read every morning: what the Night Watch found overnight, which ravens landed with a new report, what the two advisors say about one company, and your own old journal entries. Until now the 3D world could not show any of them. This step gives each one a place you walk up to, and in every case the *same* card or text from the flat page opens, so the two can never disagree.

## What you will see

- **The Night Watch post**, a post with a lantern just inside the gate, west of the road, a few steps behind the gate guard. The lantern **burns** when the watch reported in and stays **dark** when it did not. The name plate says **Night Watch !** when the night needs a look and **Night Watch ?** when the watch is not reporting or cannot be read. A watch that did not report in is never drawn as calm. Press **E**: the flat Night Watch card opens in a panel. ([post](img/game3d-s16-2-watch-post-2026-11-01.png), [panel](img/game3d-s16-2-watch-panel-2026-11-01.png))
- **The raven perch** (it already stood east of the fountain). **One raven sits on it for each raven you have not marked seen, up to three; none when there are none.** Press **E**: the flat Ravens card opens, with its own "Read the report" and "Mark as seen". Marking a raven seen here marks it on the flat page too (same per-browser list), and the bird leaves the perch. ([birds](img/game3d-s16-2-perch-birds-2026-11-01.png), [panel](img/game3d-s16-2-ravens-panel-2026-11-01.png), [bare again](img/game3d-s16-2-perch-bare-2026-11-01.png))
- **A seventh shelf in every tower library: "The Council's words"**, in the middle of the north wall. It holds the advisor lines about that holding (the same hand-written, rule-chosen lines the flat holding page lists, with "Why" facts and the disclaimer). One book per line, up to eight. It is **fog** (mist over an empty shelf) while the answer is on its way, when it could not be read, and on the sample fortress; it is a **bare shelf that says "silence is not all is well"** when no rule matched. ([shelf](img/game3d-s16-2-council-shelf-2026-11-01.png))
- **"Read the old books" at the writing desk.** The desk panel gains a button (with the count, and "N look-backs are due"). It opens your old journal entries, newest first: why you owned it, what would prove you wrong, the server's own "since then" figure, written reviews. **Read only.** The one write is the flat journal's own look-back box when a 6- or 12-month review is due. Edit and delete stay on the flat journal page ("Open the journal"). ([desk](img/game3d-s16-2-desk-2026-11-01.png), [books](img/game3d-s16-2-old-books-2026-11-01.png))

## How it works (short)

- `watchPlan.ts` (+ 15 tests): where the post and perch stand, reach, `birdCount` (one per unseen raven, at most three), `watchLook(status)` (lit or dark, plus the mark). It never works out a status or a count of changes.
- `WatchPost.tsx`, `RavenBirds.tsx`: code-built, same ink outline as everything else, no real light, no animation, so a failed model load cannot break them and reduced motion needs nothing.
- `WatchPanel.tsx`, `RavensPanel.tsx`: host the flat `NightWatchCard` and `RavensCard` as they are.
- `libraryShelves.ts`: `councilShelf(input)` and a seventh slot in `libraryRoom.ts`; the advisor lines are asked for when you walk into a tower (`GET /game/holdings/{id}/advisors`, an endpoint that already exists).
- `JournalBooksPanel.tsx`, `journalBooks.ts`: the old books. `ReviewField` in `components/Journal.tsx` is now exported and reused, so a look-back is saved by the same code and the same `PATCH` as on the flat page. No new endpoint, no backend change, no migration.
- New strings are in new version files (`watchWordsV1.ts`, `journalWordsV1.ts`).

## Decisions I took (you may overrule)

- **A watch post, not a person.** The gate guard is an existing townsperson with no behaviour; making him talk would need new animation. The post stands by him. A talking guard can replace the post later (S20 is the people decision).
- **Birds follow unseen ravens, up to three.** A fourth raven adds no bird; the panel lists them all.
- **The Council shelf's books count lines, not goodness**; its plaque tone stays plain, because the lines can differ.
- **The books live inside the desk panel's flow** (button, then a wider panel), not as a separate object, so there is one place to go for the journal.
- **Desk moved** (above).
