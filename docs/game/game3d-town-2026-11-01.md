# 3D courtyard S15 (first slice): the Town beyond the gate (2026-11-01)

**Status: written, tests and lint pass, not pushed or merged until the PR is open, not deployed, never seen in a browser.** Branch `feature/game3d-town`, off `main` at #245 (S9, S13 and all of S14 are merged).

## What a sprint is for (plain words)

The flat game has pages that are not stores: Records, the Map, the Glossary, and the plumbing (queue, status, settings, tag review). Until now you reached them only through the signpost menu. This slice gives them a place in the world: walk out of the gate, and you stand in a small Town with four buildings, one door per page.

## What you will see

- Stand on the gate road inside the south wall and a prompt says **"Press E to walk on to the Town"**. E takes you there; the courtyard is not drawn while you are in the Town, so the courtyard keeps its speed.
- The Town has a cobbled plaza with a well, and a road north to a post with a teal pennant. At the post: **"Press E to walk back to the courtyard"** (or the "← Back to the courtyard" button).
- Four buildings round the plaza, each with a name over its doormat. Stand on the mat and press E:
  - **Hall of Records** (the castle, far end): opens Records in the world panel.
  - **Cartographer** (the watchtower, west): opens the Map.
  - **Archive** (the house, east): opens the Glossary and Codex.
  - **Workshop** (the lumber mill, north-east): opens a menu of its four pages (Analysis queue, System status, Settings, Tag review).
- Every panel is the flat page itself, with the "Open the flat page" link (S13). Nothing is copied.
- If the buildings file fails to load, the Town is bare but works: ground, doormats, signs and the road back are code-built.

## Files

- `game3d/townPlan.ts` (+ `townPlan.test.ts`, 9 tests): the buildings, footprints (from measured sizes), door spots, ranges, prompts, `doorAction`. Pure.
- `game3d/Town.tsx`: ground, plaza, road, exit post, doormats with names, the four buildings and the well from `town-buildings.glb` (lazy, boundary-guarded, box colliders).
- `Scene.tsx`: `inTown` swaps the courtyard for the Town (same teleport pattern as a tower door), `NearGateWatch`. `WhereToPanel.tsx`: optional `only` place filter. `Game3DPage.tsx`: Town state, E key, prompts, button; every "near" is cleared on each crossing so a stale prompt cannot survive.
- A test checks that every register row placed in the Town or Workshop has a building behind it, so a new page for those places cannot be forgotten.
