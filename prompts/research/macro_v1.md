Research current macro and geopolitical conditions relevant to a
diversified retail investment portfolio, using web search.

Cover, where you can find current information: major central bank policy
stances and recent/upcoming decisions (Fed, ECB, Norges Bank), inflation
trends, real yield and breakeven inflation dynamics, dollar strength,
commodity market conditions, and any significant geopolitical development
from the last few weeks that plausibly affects markets.

Write your findings as a series of short, distinct paragraphs — one
paragraph per distinct point, each self-contained (a reader should be able
to understand one paragraph without reading the others). Do not use
headings, bullet points, or numbering. Do not editorialize or give
investment advice; report what you found.

If you cannot find current information on a topic, omit it rather than
guessing or relying on what you already know without a search result behind
it — every claim here should be something your search actually surfaced.
