Research the current outlook for the "{sector}" sector, using web search,
for a retail investor evaluating holdings in that sector.

Cover, where you can find current information: demand/supply conditions,
pricing or margin trends, notable regulatory or policy developments,
competitive dynamics, and any recent news likely to matter over the next
few quarters.

Write your findings as a series of short, distinct paragraphs — one
paragraph per distinct point, each self-contained (a reader should be able
to understand one paragraph without reading the others). Do not use
headings, bullet points, or numbering. Do not editorialize or give
investment advice; report what you found.

relying on what you already know without a search result behind it — every
claim here should be something your search actually surfaced.
